Questions

Good to know.

Are the links dofollow & permanent?

Yes. Every placement is a permanent, in-content dofollow link on a live article. If one ever drops, we replace it free.

How do you pick the site?

We match your niche and DR tier to a real, traffic-vetted site from our owned inventory and partner network. Every site is screened for genuine organic traffic, never an inflated DR.

What is in my report?

A full quality breakdown of your placement: Domain Rating, real organic traffic, niche relevance and spam score, so you can verify exactly what you paid for.

Do you write the content?

Yes, our team writes original articles built for the host site and your keywords. Or send your own and we place it.

Which niches do you cover?

Travel and family, health, finance and business, lifestyle, fashion and beauty, home and outdoors. Ask if yours is not listed.

Can I resell this to clients?

Absolutely. Fully white-label, clean reporting, and pricing built for reseller margin.
